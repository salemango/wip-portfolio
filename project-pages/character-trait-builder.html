Problem

How can I make a character builder app that makes picking and generating personality traits for characters a breeze?

Solution

As a hobbyist writer and off-and-on TTRPG fan, I often faced the challenge of getting to the point where I needed to develop my character further but completely blanking on what personality traits to give them. This challenge became especially frustrating when I was working on a campaign and needed to quickly generate a diverse roster of characters. When attempting to look for a tool to fix my problem and help my productivity, I was shocked to learn that there was no existing solution that met my needs and quickly got to work.

The Character Trait Builder App pulls from a JSON pseudo-database to populate its interface with a wide variety of personality traits and characteristics. These traits are sorted into color-coded positive, neutral, and negative categories for easy selection and customization. Users can simply scroll through the options and select the traits they find most fitting or interesting in order to add them to their list. A function to randomly generate traits is also available for those who want to add an element of surprise to their character development process. Vue and Axios help tie in the JSON data for a seamless user experience.
